Strategic Recommendations

1. Lead Time Management

  • Implement stricter cancellation policies for bookings made 180+ days in advance
  • Consider requiring deposits for long lead-time bookings
  • Offer incentives for non-cancellable bookings

2. Customer Segmentation

  • Prioritize group and contract customers - they have lower cancellation rates
  • Develop targeted retention strategies for transient customers
  • Create loyalty programs to convert transient to contract customers

3. Hotel Type Strategies

  • City Hotels need enhanced cancellation management - 31.4% cancellation rate is high
  • Review pricing strategies for Resort Hotels to increase ADR
  • Implement dynamic pricing based on lead time and cancellation risk

4. Revenue Protection

  • Potential revenue recovery: $8.1M by reducing cancellations
  • Focus on reducing City Hotel cancellations (highest impact - $5.95M)
  • Implement overbooking strategies based on customer type and lead time
